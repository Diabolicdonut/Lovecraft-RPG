## Approved full vision

A persistent, browser-based Lovecraftian RPG using Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition behind-the-screen mechanics, an optional detailed investigator sheet and intelligible task-success previews, a structured AI Keeper, coherent procedurally generated adventures and campaigns, optional HTML scene maps/character positions, and later optional licensed sound. Official scenarios are private references and prospective user-local compatible modules, not content for redistribution in the public repository.

**Whole-product accepted: 18 of 100 weighted points (18%)**, across **23 evidence gates**; **separate CoC transition phase: 0/5**. Lower percentage than historical 35% is caused by adding significant new scope, not losing working features.
